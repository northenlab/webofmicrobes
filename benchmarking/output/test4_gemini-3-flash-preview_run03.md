To identify the preferred substrates and Binary Preference Ratio (BPR) scores, we look to metabolic reconstructions (such as the AGORA collection) and experimental fermentation data. 

The **BPR (Binary Preference Ratio)** score typically ranges from **0 to 1**, where **1.0** represents a primary, highly efficient substrate for growth, and **0.0** represents non-utilization.
