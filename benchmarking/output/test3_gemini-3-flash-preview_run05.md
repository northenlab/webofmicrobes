### Step 1: Doubling Time Prediction and Filtering
In this step, the doubling time for each genus is predicted based on optimal laboratory conditions (typically $30$-$37^\circ\text{C}$ in nutrient-rich or standardized minimal media). Genera with a predicted doubling time greater than 5 hours are filtered out.

**Genera removed (Doubling time > 5 hours):**
*   **Afipia:** ~12–24 hours
*   **Bradyrhizobium:** ~7–12 hours
*   **Marmoricola:** ~10–15 hours
*   **Mesorhizobium:** ~6–10 hours
*   **Methylobacterium:** ~7–10 hours
*   **Modestobacter:** ~24–48+ hours

Competitiveness is determined by the presence of efficient xylose transport systems (e.g., ABC transporters), the metabolic pathway used (Isomerase vs. Weimberg), and the lack of severe carbon catabolite repression (CCR) when xylose is the primary substrate.
