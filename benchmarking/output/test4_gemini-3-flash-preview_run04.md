To determine the preferred substrates and provide BPR (Binary Phenotype Result/Biochemical Potential) scores, we look to metabolic reconstruction data (such as the AGORA database) and experimental fermentation studies. 

The **BPR score** in this context represents the predicted growth capability or metabolic flux on a scale of **0 to 1**, where 0 is no utilization and 1 is optimal utilization.
