Ranking substrate preference for *Pseudomonas* (specifically the model organism *Pseudomonas aeruginosa*) is complex because, unlike *E. coli*, *Pseudomonas* exhibits **Reverse Carbon Catabolite Repression (CCR)**. It prefers organic acids and specific amino acids over glucose.

This ranking is based on metabolic hierarchy studies (e.g., Palmer et al., 2007; Frimmersdorf et al., 2010) and the efficiency of the Entner-Doudoroff (ED) pathway versus amino acid catabolism.

### The Ranking (Highest Preference to Lowest)

1.  **Glutamate** (Top tier; enters the TCA cycle directly via α-ketoglutarate; primary C and N source)
2.  **Proline** (Highly preferred; rapidly converted to glutamate)
3.  **Arginine** (Major C/N source; *Pseudomonas* has multiple pathways to utilize it)
4.  **4-guanidinobutanoic acid** (An intermediate of arginine/proline metabolism; highly preferred by *Pseudomonas*)
5.  **Alanine** (Easily converted to pyruvate)
6.  **Serine** (Directly converted to pyruvate; high priority)
7.  **Glucose** (The preferred sugar, but repressed by the amino acids above)
8.  **Mannitol** (Polyol; very efficiently metabolized via the ED pathway)
9.  **Fructose** (Enters the ED pathway, but usually slightly behind glucose/mannitol)
10. **Mannose** (Utilized well, but requires more processing than glucose)
11. **Phenylalanine** (Aromatic amino acid; *Pseudomonas* are specialists in aromatics, but it's energetically costly)
12. **Leucine** (Branched-chain amino acid; enters via Acetyl-CoA)
13. **Isoleucine** (Branched-chain amino acid; similar to leucine)
14. **Threonine** (Degraded to glycine and acetyl-CoA)
15. **Glycine** (Simple, but less energetically favorable than larger amino acids)
16. **Lysine** (Slow degradation pathway compared to other amino acids)
17. **Adenosine** (Nucleoside; used as a nitrogen source, but carbon preference is lower)
18. **Uridine** (Nucleoside; similar to adenosine)
19. **Hypoxanthine** (Purine base; requires significant processing)
20. **Guanine** (Purine base; low carbon value)
21. **Arabinose** (Pentose sugar; many *Pseudomonas* strains utilize this poorly or slowly)
22. **Trehalose** (Disaccharide; low preference/slow induction of transporters)
23. **Maltose** (Often at the bottom; many *P. aeruginosa* strains lack the high-affinity transport systems for maltose found in enteric bacteria)

---

### Key Logic for this Ranking:

*   **The "Organic Acid/Amino Acid" Rule:** *Pseudomonas* prioritizes substrates that feed directly into the TCA cycle (Glutamate, Proline, Arginine) over sugars. This is the opposite of *E. coli*.
*   **The Sugar Hierarchy:** Among sugars, *Pseudomonas* uses the Entner-Doudoroff pathway. Glucose and Mannitol are the "gold standards" for sugars, while pentoses (Arabinose) and disaccharides (Maltose/Trehalose) are generally poorly utilized or repressed.
*   **Nitrogen Value:** Amino acids like Arginine and Glutamate are ranked higher because they provide both high-quality carbon skeletons and readily available nitrogen.
*   **Nucleosides vs. Bases:** Nucleosides (Adenosine/Uridine) are generally preferred over free bases (Guanine/Hypoxanthine) because the ribose/deoxyribose sugar provides an immediate carbon hit.
