### Step 1: Predict Doubling Times and Filter Genera
In this step, the doubling time (DT) for each genus was predicted based on typical growth rates in optimal laboratory conditions (e.g., nutrient-rich media at 25–37°C). Genera with a doubling time greater than 5 hours were removed.

**Genera Removed (Predicted Doubling Time > 5 hours):**
*   **Afipia:** ~12–24 hours
*   **Bradyrhizobium:** ~7–14 hours
*   **Lysobacter:** ~6–10 hours
*   **Marmoricola:** ~10–15 hours
*   **Mesorhizobium:** ~6–12 hours
*   **Methylobacterium:** ~6–12 hours
*   **Modestobacter:** >24 hours
*   **Mucilaginibacter:** ~6–8 hours
*   **Niastella:** ~8–15 hours
*   **Phenylobacterium:** ~10–20 hours
*   **Streptomyces:** ~5.5–8 hours
