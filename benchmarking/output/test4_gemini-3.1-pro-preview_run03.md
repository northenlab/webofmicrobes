To selectively enrich *Bacillus* species from soil while suppressing *Pedobacter*, you must exploit the fundamental physiological differences between the two genera. 

*Bacillus* species are Gram-positive, spore-forming, highly versatile facultative anaerobes (or robust aerobes), and are generally halotolerant (salt-tolerant). *Pedobacter* species are Gram-negative, strictly aerobic, non-spore-forming, and are generally sensitive to high salt and specific organic acids.

Here are the specific metabolites and chemical strategies you should use to favor *Bacillus* over *Pedobacter*:

### 1. Selective Carbon Sources
*Pedobacter* species prefer complex structural polysaccharides (like heparin, chondroitin, and xylan) or simple sugars (glucose, maltose). They generally lack the transport mechanisms for certain organic acids and sugar alcohols that *Bacillus* thrives on.
*   **Citrate (e.g., Sodium Citrate):** Many *Bacillus* species (like *B. subtilis* and *B. licheniformis*) possess citrate permease and can utilize citrate as a sole carbon source. Most *Pedobacter* species are citrate-negative.
*   **Mannitol or Sorbitol:** *Bacillus* species are highly efficient at utilizing and fermenting sugar alcohols like mannitol. *Pedobacter* species generally do not utilize mannitol, making it an excellent selective carbon source.
*   **Glycerol:** While some *Pedobacter* might weakly utilize glycerol, *Bacillus* metabolizes it rapidly. When combined with a selective nitrogen source, it heavily favors *Bacillus*.

### 2. Selective Nitrogen Sources & Electron Acceptors
*Pedobacter* typically requires organic nitrogen (like peptones or yeast extract) for robust growth. *Bacillus* can easily synthesize its own amino acids from inorganic nitrogen.
*   **Nitrate (e.g., Potassium Nitrate, KNO₃):** This is a dual-purpose metabolite. *Bacillus* can use nitrate both as a nitrogen source and as a **terminal electron acceptor** for anaerobic respiration. Because *Pedobacter* is strictly aerobic, adding nitrate and incubating the soil slurry in microaerophilic or anaerobic conditions (e.g., in a sealed bottle or deep liquid culture) will completely halt *Pedobacter* growth while *Bacillus* flourishes.
*   **Urea:** Many soil *Bacillus* species are strongly urease-positive and can use urea as a sole nitrogen source. *Pedobacter* species are generally urease-negative.

### 3. Osmolytes (Salt Stress)
While not a carbon/nitrogen metabolite, manipulating the osmotic environment is the most reliable way to separate these two.
*   **Sodium Chloride (NaCl) at 5% to 7%:** *Bacillus* species are famously halotolerant, with many soil strains easily growing in 5–10% NaCl. *Pedobacter* species are highly sensitive to osmotic stress and are typically inhibited at NaCl concentrations above 2% to 3%. 

### 4. Natural Inhibitors
*   **Phenylethyl Alcohol (PEA):** If you are willing to use a selective chemical additive, PEA is a volatile alcohol that disrupts the outer membrane of Gram-negative bacteria (like *Pedobacter*) while leaving Gram-positive bacteria (like *Bacillus*) intact.

---

### Recommended Enrichment Strategy (The "Bacillus Favor" Broth)
To practically apply this to your soil sample, create an enrichment medium using the following combination:

1.  **Carbon Source:** 10 g/L Mannitol or Sodium Citrate (Avoid glucose or yeast extract, which *Pedobacter* loves).
2.  **Nitrogen Source:** 2 g/L Potassium Nitrate (KNO₃) or Urea.
3.  **Osmotic Agent:** 50 g/L NaCl (5% salt).
4.  **Minerals:** Standard minimal salts (Magnesium sulfate, Potassium phosphate).
5.  **Incubation:** Incubate at **37°C** (Many *Pedobacter* are psychrotolerant and prefer 15–25°C, whereas *Bacillus* thrives at 30–37°C). 

**The Ultimate Physical "Cheat Code": Heat Shock**
Because you are working with soil, the absolute easiest way to guarantee *Bacillus* enrichment and zero *Pedobacter* survival is to pasteurize your soil suspension. Before adding the soil to your enrichment broth, heat the soil-water slurry to **80°C for 15 minutes**. This will kill all vegetative cells (including 100% of *Pedobacter*) while the *Bacillus* endospores survive and subsequently germinate in your selective metabolite broth.
