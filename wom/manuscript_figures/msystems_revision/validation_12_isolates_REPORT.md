## Summary

1. **Fig. S3 was not produced by the code in the original hypothesis.** It came from
   `figure_8_experimental_validation_single_model()` in
   `wom/scripts/make_figures_for_manuscript_nature_microbiology_first_submission.py`, reading
   `wom/exometabolite_data/validation/held-out-validation-data.tsv`. The `~/Downloads/ExoW{2,3,4}_…Dunnet.xlsx`
   and `phylogeny - Isolates (3).tsv` files do not exist anywhere under the home directory, and
   were not inputs to the 12-isolate figure.
2. **Fig. S3 already uses the revised label definitions.** Consumed is ≤ −2, neutral is −2 < v < +2, and secreted
   values are excluded. Serratia is included through an order-level fallback. Audit items 3a–3d
   describe an older genus-level precision@k figure, not Fig. S3.
3. **Two of the 12 "held-out" isolates are in the training data.** These are *Delftia* GW456-R20 and *Cupriavidus*
   FW507-4G11, from `wom_1.tsv` and `wom_8.tsv`. Retraining without them barely changes any AUC
   (largest change: Delftia −0.013).
4. **The reported mean ROC-AUC of 0.87 is not reproduced.** The submitted run's own per-isolate AUCs average **0.887**.
   "11/12 ≥ 0.8" and "Caulobacter lowest (0.77)" do match the submitted run. However, "11/12" depends on the
   random seed (the count ranges from 9 to 12 across seeds), because Agrobacterium and one Pseudomonas sit right at 0.80.
5. **Corrected, genuinely held-out estimate.** We trained 20 seeded models with both in-training isolates removed. Their mean ROC-AUC is
   **0.883 ± 0.004** (SD across seeds; range 0.875–0.889), the median is 0.888, and 10–11 of 12 isolates reach ≥ 0.8.
   Caulobacter is lowest in 19/20 seeds (0.749 ± 0.033).

Suggested wording for the revision, if the clean hold-out models are adopted:
"…12 newly profiled NLDM isolates absent from training: mean ROC-AUC 0.88 (median 0.89; 20 independently
initialised models, SD 0.004), with 10–11 of 12 isolates ≥ 0.80 depending on initialisation; *Caulobacter*
was lowest (0.75)." The revised Fig. S3 (`figS3_validation_clean_holdout.png`) is drawn from these clean
models. The submitted figure is left unchanged.

## 2. Audit (items 3a–3e)

**3a. Consumed threshold.** In the ordinal / precision@k code it is `value < -2` (strict). **In the code that produced Fig. S3 it is
`value <= -2`**, which matches the manuscript. 33 of the 831 validation values are exactly −2, so the choice
matters (see §4).

**3b. Neutral definition.** In the ordinal / precision@k code, neutral is `~consumed`, so secreted values (≥ +2) count as neutral. **In the code that produced Fig. S3,
neutral is `(value > -2) & (value < 2)`. Secreted values are excluded from both the histograms and the AUC.** This is already the
revised definition. 21 values are ≥ +2.

**3c. Serratia.**
- **Origin.** `bad_genera = ['Deinococcus', 'Serratia']` was introduced in `a53fb20` (2026-03-02, "added testing").
  That commit added the whole of `make_figures_for_manuscript.py`, with no comment or commit message giving a reason.
- **Likely reason (inferred, not documented).** Neither genus has training isolates at genus level. Serratia has none at family level (Yersiniaceae) either. So
  the old genus-only merge could not score them, and they would have dropped out silently anyway.
- **In Fig. S3.** `bad_genera` was removed in `8211f2f`. **Fig. S3 contains 12 isolates (12 panels), and Serratia MT49 is included**.
  It is scored at order level: Enterobacterales, whose training isolates are only *Erwinia* GW821-FHT05B06 and
  *E. coli* BW25113. The validation file contains no Deinococcus.

**3d. Taxa with no training isolates.** In the ordinal / precision@k code, the merge is on genus only, so such isolates drop out silently. **The code that produced Fig. S3 falls back
genus → family → order.** Two isolates need the fallback:

| Isolate | Genus training isolates | Rank used | Reference taxon | n training isolates |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Agrobacterium GW821-FHT02D03 | 0 | family | Rhizobiaceae | 8 |
| Serratia MT49 | 0 (family also 0) | order | Enterobacterales | 2 |

All others use genus level. Note that *Acinetobacter* rests on a single training isolate, and *Ensifer* and
*Paraburkholderia* on two each.

**3e. Were the isolates absent from training?**
- **`numerical_index >= 224` cannot be checked.** It indexes into the missing `phylogeny - Isolates (3).tsv`, and Fig. S3
  does not use it.
- **How the IDs were checked instead.** All 12 validation IDs were searched in `exometabolite.parquet` `organism_id`. The search used exact matches,
  case- and punctuation-normalised matches, and substring matches, and was repeated against the saved model's `microbe_to_idx`.
- **Result: 10 isolates are absent. Two are present:**

| Isolate | Genus | Training source files | Training isolates incl. itself |
|---|---|---|---|
| GW456-R20 | Delftia | `wom_1.tsv`, `wom_8.tsv` | 3 (2 without it) |
| FW507-4G11 | Cupriavidus | `wom_1.tsv` | 8 (7 without it) |

The validation measurements for these two isolates are a new profiling run, not copies of the training rows: only 53–72% of shared
compounds have identical values. They are still correlated with the training profiles of the same isolates (Pearson r = 0.62–0.86). The parquet
(2026-03-25) predates the Fig. S3 run (2026-05-05), so the Fig. S3 model was trained on both isolates. The panel counts
"Delftia (3)" and "Cupriavidus (8)" include the isolate itself. MT49 appears in `phylogeny.tsv` (taxonomy only) but has
no measurements in any training source.

## 3. Does the reproduction match the reported numbers?

| Claim in manuscript | Submitted run (`figure8_auc_per_isolate.csv`) | Saved final model | 20 seeded retrains, same training data |
|---|---|---|---|
| mean ROC-AUC = 0.87 | **0.887** | 0.881 | 0.883 (SD 0.007; range 0.869–0.894) |
| 11/12 ≥ 0.8 | 11/12 | 9/12 | 9/12 in 1 seed, 10/12 in 10, 11/12 in 7, 12/12 in 2 |
| Caulobacter lowest, 0.77 | 0.773, lowest | 0.761, lowest | 0.747 ± 0.047; lowest in 16/20 seeds |

- **Matches:** "11/12 ≥ 0.8" and "Caulobacter 0.77" match the submitted run exactly.
- **Does not match:** the mean of that run is 0.887, not 0.87. No submitted per-isolate output gives 0.87. Across seeds the mean
  reaches ≤ 0.875 in only 2 of 20 runs, so 0.87 may come from an earlier unsaved run or be a transcription error. It cannot be traced.
- **The ≥ 0.8 count is not robust.** Agrobacterium (≥ 0.8 in 45% of seeds) and Pseudomonas GW458-11-26-14-LB3 (90%) sit at the threshold.
  Caulobacter reaches 0.8 in 15%.

**Legacy labels (< −2 vs everything else) → corrected labels, saved model.** The legacy labels were never used for Fig. S3.
They would have inflated the result to a mean of 0.899 and 12/12 ≥ 0.8.

**Submitted run → saved model, same labels; the only difference is the model instance:**
- Pseudomonas GW458-11-26-14-LB3: 0.835 → 0.794 (−0.041)
- Serratia MT49: 0.894 → 0.919 (+0.025)

Per-isolate SD across seeds is 0.008–0.047, so differences of this size are within run-to-run variation.

**Full-data retrains → clean hold-out (mean of 20 seeds):** no isolate changes by more than 0.02. The largest change is
Delftia, 0.896 → 0.883 (−0.013). Cupriavidus changes by −0.000. Including these two isolates in training did not
materially inflate their scores.

## 6. Caveats

- **Ensifer has a single neutral compound** under the corrected labels (65 consumed / 1 neutral). Its ROC-AUC is the rank of
  one compound and should not be weighted like the others.
- **Prevalence is high (mean 0.75, up to 0.99).** PR-AUC must be read against this baseline. ROC-AUC is the more interpretable metric here.
- **Serratia is scored against an order-level reference of two unrelated genera** (*Erwinia*, *E. coli*). Acinetobacter's
  genus reference is one isolate.
- **Brevundimonas is missing from the 12-isolate set.** The 8-isolate first submission included it, with a per-genus AUC of 0.75 in
  `manuscript_figures_nat_microbiology_first_submission/figure8_auc_per_genus.csv`. It is absent from
  `held-out-validation-data.tsv`. The reason is not recorded. A reviewer comparing versions may ask.
- **The original `~/Downloads` data files are gone.** The 12-isolate validation TSV, which is the Fig. S3 input, is now tracked in git
  (`.gitignore` negation). The raw training sources in `wom/exometabolite_data/` (`wom_*.tsv`, `phylogeny.tsv`) remain untracked.
  The formatted training table `wom/processed_data/exometabolite.parquet` is tracked.

## Reproduce

```bash
cd wom
.venv/bin/python scripts/revision_validation_12_isolates.py            # 20 seeds per training set, ~4 min
.venv/bin/python scripts/revision_validation_12_isolates.py --n-seeds 5
.venv/bin/python scripts/revision_figS3_clean_holdout.py               # revised Fig. S3, ~2.5 min; needs the table above
```

Seeds 0–19 are set via `np.random.seed` and `torch.manual_seed`. Seeded runs are bit-for-bit reproducible on CPU.
